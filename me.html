The real me

Beyond the code

Here the screen goes quiet. This is the part of me that does not compile: art, music, long days at the gym and a slow life.

Wander slowly

01 — WonderWhat I love

Art

I do not paint, I look. I can stand for hours in front of a painting, and Van Gogh's swirling skies make even movement feel calm.

Music

Music is the soundtrack of my days. It follows me when I study, when I train and when I do nothing at all.

The gym

I can shut myself in the gym for whole days. Between weights and effort, my mind finally goes quiet.

Slow life

I choose the slow life: no rush, small things done with care, and time that is not measured in deadlines.

I often think that the night is more alive and more richly coloured than the day.

Vincent van Gogh

Thank you for staying a little. Take the quiet with you.
